Global monitoring platform rollout

Technology transformation · Global · Team leadership
Challenge
Thousands of managed and franchised properties had inconsistent visibility and alerting, with no single standard for how IT issues were detected.
What I did
Owned the roadmap and delivery of a global observability platform and led the team behind it. Sequenced the rollout by regional data volume and local IT manager readiness. Ran workshops with property IT managers to gather feedback, built what was needed, dropped what wasn't, and contacted properties directly where no contact team existed.
Outcome
6,000+ managed and franchised hotels onboarded, with standardised visibility and alerting. Adoption and success KPIs, runbooks and dashboards enabled proactive monitoring and faster MTTR.

AIOps roadmap and guest-experience observability

Generative AI · Vendor management · Data & reporting
Challenge
Leaders needed to know how the guest-facing digital experience was performing, and whether AI-assisted operations could reduce alert noise, without a clear path to get AI approved.
What I did
Ran synthetic and user-journey monitoring for end-to-end visibility of the digital experience, plus log monitoring with OpenTelemetry. Defined first-year wins and a 3-5 year capability roadmap for the vendor's AI engine, shared it with the vendor, and drove the governance that led to approval. Built Tableau dashboards for reliability trends, SLA adherence and deployment performance.
Outcome
AI capability approved for adoption, with planning under way to route its alerts into the ITSM platform. Leadership gained dashboards they could act on.
